ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Growing curious minds into confident individuals.

Genius Children Senior Secondary School, Parbatsar, is dedicated to creating an engaging learning environment where students can develop academically, personally and socially.

Our approach brings together classroom learning, activities, creativity, discipline and opportunities for students to discover their strengths.
